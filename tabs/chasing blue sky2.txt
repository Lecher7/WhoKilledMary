Chasing Blue Sky
----------------

Performed by Queensrÿche
From the Hear In the Now Frontier CD  (B-side)




You really need the Japanese improt CD, or one of the singles so that you can hear this
song to get the timing down, etc.......

These are the basic chords to the song:


D minor.....xx0321 (open A optional)
C Major.....x32010
G Major.....3x003x
A/F ???.....x03010


The progression basically goes in that order.  Nice mellow tune, if you have the chance to get
it on CD (or a copy), get it!

Enjoy!
