Title: Two Miles High
Album: Promised Land CD-ROM Game
Transcribed by: Raven
Email: grans@earthlink.net

This is the song at the end of the "Promised Land" game, where the band 
plays a song to reward you for finishing the quest. Thanks to Sacred 
Ground's Queensryche Emporium, from which the sites Real Audio Archive I 
was able to transcribe this wondeful tune. Obviously, you can strum the 
chords however you wish. If somebody out there has the MP3 for this 
song, please, I would be ever so greatful if you send me a copy.  RYCHE 
ON!!
