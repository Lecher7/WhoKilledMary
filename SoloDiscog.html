Solo Releases

Here is where you will find information on solo material and side projects by current and past members of Queensryche.



Geoff Tate ******* Geoff Tate ***** Format: CD
Vocalist Geoff Tate's first solo album outside Queensrÿche could, if promoted correctly, finally draw attention to a stellar voice that has gone unheard outside the metal lexicon. Although he keeps in check the tenor he can magnificently tap, Tate artfully flexes his five-octave range over 11 cuts that bring together electronica atmospheres and hard-rock passion surrounded by textures and harmonies that recall Queensrÿche's The Promised Land.
[ Order it ]

Rockenfield/Speer ******* Hell's Canyon ***** Format: CD
The thunderously stormy opening of the track "Descent" lets the listener know that the musical journey that they are about to embark upon will be one of high speed thrills, mixed in with a rare piece of calm. Gutsy guitar action on "Seven Devils" and "Coyote" will reform listeners that are not ordinarily fans of electric guitar led compositions. Subtle and clever use of compression and echo effects on the guitar tracks of quieter compositions make this CD interesting to listen to.
[ Order it ]

Rockenfield/Speer ******* Hell's Canyon (Euro release) ***** Format: CD
The European release contains the bonus track Epilogue, as well as having alternate cover art.
[ Order it ]

Rockenfield/Speer ******* Televoid ***** Format: VHS/DVD
A computer-animated science-fiction fable from director Michael Boydstun, TeleVoid is about a television channel from hell that literally sucks your mind away and how Skully tries to fight the video demon. Features the song Chasing Blue Sky, originally released by Queensryche on Hear In The Now Frontier. This highly acclaimed first outing from Rockenfield/Speer is a computer animation thrill ride that received a Grammy nomination in 1998 for Long Form Music Video.
[ Order it ]

Slave To The System ******* Slave To The System ***** Format: CD
Highly anticipated release featuring Scott Rockenfield and Kelly Gray, along with Scott Heard, Damon Johnson and Roman Glick. The beginnings of the band come from this news article posted on Damon's site on October 26, 2000: In other brother news as of late, this past Saturday [10/21] both Roman Glick and Damon headed up to Seattle to do some writing with Wishpool producer and Queensryche guitarist Kelly Gray. They'll be in town for the week helping Kelly on some tunes for a record that he's doing with 'Ryche drummer Scott Rockenfield. Damon says, "I'm stoked to work with Roman again and drink lots of Seattle coffee!!!!!!!!"
[ Order it ]

Spys4Darwin (Degarmo) ******* Microfiche ***** Format: CD
Chris DeGarmo, former guitarist of Queensryche, Vin Dombroski, lead singer of Sponge, and Mike Inez & Sean Kinney, the rhythm section of Alice In Chains, came to be in the dark confines of a Seattle rehearsal space in late 1999. The sessions have continued, with a portion of those first weeks together emerging as microfiche, and additional material slated for a full-length album.
[ Order it ]
