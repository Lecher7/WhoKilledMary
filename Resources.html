Software for Collectors:

I looked for some software that assists you in keeping track of your collection, and the only one I found that seemed to be decent was Tapetracker. However, it costs $60 to register, and being a programmer I knew it could be done cheaper. So, I created TapeBase for half the price. Give 'em both a shot and see which suits your needs better.
