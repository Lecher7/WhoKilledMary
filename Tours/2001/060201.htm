Christopher E. Goettle: The night was June 2nd, 2001. It was to be a day I'll never
forget as long as I shall live. The place was The Seattle showbox ,
located in downtown Seattle. Time was 10:00pm. So there I was ,
just another face in the crowd, approximately 10 feet away at the most
from the center stage.
The lights went out, and Scott Rockenfields drum neon light was on.
Something was about to happen. Then there they were: Geoff Tate,
Micheal Wilton, Eddie Jackson, Scott Rockenfield, and Kelly Gray
coming on stage. The crowd exploded in excitement , and the place was
filled with a couple hundred Ryche-heads applauding there presence.
Geoff offered his greetings to us all, and thanked everyone for
showing up to see him and his band play. He started off with the
early years hits, "Enforce", then followed with the over powering
performence of "No Sanctuary", one of the songs which he needed our
help being the back up vocals, ( which on the c-d is done by the
Micheal Kamen orchestra ), then carried on with " The Lady Wore
Black", which brought back many memories of their first self titled
album, jumped into the "Empire" mode, then once again Geoff
needed some "help " with the every so popular "Take Hold Of The Flame"
, a song which is a fan favorite, we all sang our parts in it, and
the way Geoff performs at his shows, it is very enjoyable seeing his
band play!
Geoff then told us how he was writing one of the first songs for the
first full-length c-d, and said "this is a song of a few bits and
pieces form other songs " and came up with "NM156" , which he does a
great job of changing the sound of his voice into the sound of a
man-machine speaking during 3 small takes in the song . From there
Geoff said "We are going in to the Mindcrime album, then came
"The Mission" then a beautiful duet with Pamela Moore on "Suite
Sister Mary" ( which I personally have been playing in my car
thinking I would never get to see that live) , the two of them sound
so good together , you just have to experience it to believe it !!
Then came the newest releases, " Sacred Ground", "The Right Side Of
My Mind", "Liquid Sky", followed by the sizzling performence of
"Breakdown", and "Queen of the Ryche" squeezed in the act . After the
show was over, we (us the fans) chanted Ryche Ryche Ryche, by that
time the equipment people were on stage to disconnect the bands
instruments, then after a few moments, There I was, in the middle
of the Showbox, waiting to see what was gonna happen next. I had
never had a close encounter with anyone famous or anything, so while
I was waiting, and nothing to do, I was just talked to a few Ryche
fans, and there he was, Eddie Jackson, the best bassist I have ever
seen, Eddie was walking towards me, with his security, then he stopped to
sign autographs, man, this guy was a little shorter then I am, and
I asked him for his autograph, he asked me where could he sign, I
said anywhere you want on my shirt, ( cuz I didnt have anyhting else) ,
so he just laughed a second, and says he is gonna sign my left tit!!!
It was funny, Then some dude form a couple feet away says thats a $50
shirt you got on, I told him it was ok, its the RYCHE!!! Ok it gets
better, me and some guy were looking for Geoff, and I spotted him
sitting at the bar so we went over there to get smooshed in the crowd
wating to meet thim. I never seen a celebrity on tv, or anything
be as kind as he was, pure gentlemen. Took the time to greet every
single person, signed autographs for them, and took pictures with the
fans. Then , there I am, no one knew who was next in line to meet him,
so he looked at me, and I didnt know what to really say or do, so I
was gonna hand him my marker to sign my shirt, but he put out his hand
to greet me, ME!!!!! Me , as in some guy who practically uses his
music as mental therepy on a day to day basis to get through the day!!
ha ha ha ... I shook his hand, and said, "It's a pleasure to meet
you" and "Could I have a picture? Geoff said , "Sure"!!!! He took the
camera out of my hand, and took a snap shot of me!!!!! The crowd
laughed a little, I said "No, I ment of me and you!!!" Geoff said "
YES!!! !!!" HE TOLD ME YES!!! So I put my on his shoulder, (man
this was the best feeling I ever had)!!! Some lady in the crowd took
that picture and I was about as happy as you could ever see me!!!! I
looked around, and found Kelly Gray, not very many people were around
him , so I ran to him, and he is way cool!!! He signed my shirt as
well as Eddie and Geoff, and then said yes also to have a picture
with me also!! I was like, , I think I'm dreaming!!! So Kelly put
his arm around me, and I tried to him but my hand got caught up in his
hair!! It was kind of funny, I told him he has a lot of hair!! He
laughed, and the picture was taken!!!!
I saw Micheal Wilton mobbed practically, and Scott Rockenfield was not
seen anwhere. I didn't bother going towards Micheal knowing it would
be a long while to get to meet him, and I was very disappointed in
Scott. .....You know, I here good and bad bout alot of rock bands ,
and yes I heard lot of bad bout the Rcyhe. But I'll tell you, they are
the coolest, fan appreciative, and took time to get personal, they
are just a great group of guys . It was the most awesome thing to
experience, and to know my favorite group was so cool, and took the
extra minute to meet every person, it was just a awesome feeling to
be able to meet them. And I got a couple email addys , one for which
was (Rory) from mageditor@queensryche.com . I initially thought he
was just another guy who liked the ryche as much as me, and to think I
met someone who works for them!!!! YEAHHHHHHHH hehehheeh . Well, that
was night in Seattle . I really hope you enjoy reading this, I tried
to make it as , as if you were there to feel what it was like as you
read this. By the way, of all the negativity I've heard about Kelly
Gray, I'll say you can't never take away what Chris DeGarmo meant
to the band, but Kelly is a awesome performer, he knows he cant
take Chris's place, and he works hard , plays hard, and does the best
job he can do, and he proved that with all the songs the Ryche
performed.To be honest, Geoff Tate is a very intelligent person, and a
perfectionist at that, I really dont see Geoff just getting a
"someone" to play guitar for for his band, Kelly must have done
something right to get his job, and I like him alot, and he is just a
nice as Geoff and Eddie were to me.Let me know what you think, I'll
redo if you would like.
Ryche-fully yours,

Christopher E. Goettle
